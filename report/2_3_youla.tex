Youla Parameterization

We are given a plant 

\[
    G(s) = \frac{s-1}{s^3 + s^2 - 4s - 4},
\]
which has a zero at $s=1$ and poles at $s=-2,\,s=-1,\,s=2$.

\begin{lstlisting}
s = tf('s');
G = (s-1)/(s^3 + s^2 - 4*s - 4);

[A, B, C, D] = tf2ss(G.num{1}, G.den{1});
\end{lstlisting}

Now, letscreate a F controller 

\[
    F = -place(A, B, [-2, -1, 2]);
\]
